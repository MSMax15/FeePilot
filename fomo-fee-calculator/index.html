Three things to remember.

01

Spot has a floor.

FOMO's current terms say 0.50% per transaction with a $0.95 minimum fee. Small trades can therefore have a higher effective percentage cost.

02

Perps have extra layers.

FOMO currently lists a 0.05% fee for perps, in addition to fees or funding charged by the underlying third-party protocols.

03

Referral terms can move.

FOMO referral pages currently advertise 10% off fees. Always confirm the benefit shown by FOMO before you trade.
