Why small trades feel different

The minimum matters.

On a $100 transaction, 0.50% equals $0.50, but the current $0.95 minimum would make the effective FOMO fee 0.95% before any other costs. That is why a flat percentage alone does not tell the whole story.

FOMO fee FAQ

The questions people usually ask next.

Are FOMO fees hidden?

The platform fee structure is published in FOMO's Terms, while the Terms also say third-party fees can apply and may not be disclosed by FOMO. Check the live transaction flow and current terms before acting.

Does LMP506 remove the minimum fee?

We do not claim that it does. FOMO's referral page currently advertises 10% off fees; the exact live calculation and eligibility should be verified in FOMO.

Are funding fees the same as trading fees?

No. Apple Pay, debit-card, crypto-network or other third-party funding costs are separate layers from the FOMO platform fee described here.
