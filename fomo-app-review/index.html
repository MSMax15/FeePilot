FOMO app review: what it does well — and what to watch

FOMO is positioned as a social crypto trading app focused on fast discovery and simple execution. Its official site highlights feeds, leaderboards, alerts, easy onboarding, gasless multichain access and one-click buying. It now also supports perpetual futures.

What stands out

  • Social discovery: feeds and trader-following features can make market activity easier to discover.
  • Simple onboarding: the product is designed to reduce wallet and chain complexity for newer users.
  • Broad product range: FOMO is not meme-only; it also supports major crypto and perps.

What costs to know

Current FOMO Terms state a minimum 0.50% fee per buy/sell transaction, subject to a $0.95 minimum. Perps carry a 0.05% FOMO fee plus possible third-party trading, gas and funding costs. Fees can change.

Referral offer

FOMO's public referral pages currently advertise 10% off fees for entering a referral code in-app. SaveOnFees features LMP506. Confirm the current benefit in FOMO before trading.

Main downsides and risks

  • Fast social discovery can encourage impulsive trading.
  • Meme coins can lose most or all of their value.
  • Perpetuals introduce leverage and liquidation risk.
  • Transaction minimum fees can matter on very small trades.
Best fit: users who value a simplified social trading interface and understand the costs and risks. Beginners should start with spot concepts before considering leverage.
