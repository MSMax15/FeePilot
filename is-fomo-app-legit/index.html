Is the FOMO app legit?

What we can verify: FOMO operates an official website and app ecosystem, publishes Terms of Service and fee disclosures, documents its wallet/security architecture, runs a referral program and currently offers spot crypto plus perpetual futures through third-party protocols.

That does not mean every trade, token or product is safe. “Legit platform” and “safe investment” are different questions.

Things you should verify yourself

  • You are on the official fomo.family domain or official app listing.
  • The fee shown before the transaction matches what you expect.
  • Your jurisdiction is eligible for the product.
  • You understand custody, withdrawals and private-key access.
  • You understand the token or perpetual contract you are buying.

Security information FOMO publishes

FOMO says its wallet uses an embedded self-custodial architecture and documents biometric protections such as Face ID for app access, withdrawals and private-key export on supported Apple devices.

Important risk distinction

Meme coins remain highly speculative. FOMO's own educational content tells users to assume money put into memecoins could go to zero. Perpetuals add leverage, funding and liquidation risk.

Bottom line: verify the platform, but evaluate every trade separately. SaveOnFees does not certify FOMO or any token as risk-free.
