FOMO perpetual fees explained

Current FOMO fee: FOMO's Terms state a 0.05% fee per Perps transaction.

That is not necessarily your total cost. The Terms also say third-party protocols can charge additional trading fees, gas fees and funding-rate payments.

Simple example

At a 0.05% FOMO fee, a $10,000 transaction would equal a $5 FOMO fee before any separate protocol or funding costs. This is only a fee illustration, not a recommendation to trade.

Perps are different from spot

Perpetual futures are leveraged derivative products. FOMO's current product materials say perps include crypto, equity, index, commodity and pre-IPO contracts through third-party protocols. Perps are not available to U.S. Persons and may be restricted elsewhere.

What to check before placing a perps trade

  • The live FOMO fee shown for the trade.
  • Any third-party protocol fee.
  • Funding payments.
  • Leverage and liquidation price.
  • Whether the product is available in your jurisdiction.
High risk: leverage can amplify losses. A lower trading fee does not reduce liquidation risk.
