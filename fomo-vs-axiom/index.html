Published spot fee comparison

FOMO's headline platform fee is lower; trade context still matters.

FOMO

0.50% minimum

FOMO's Terms dated July 15, 2026 state a minimum 0.50% fee per buy/sell transaction, subject to a $0.95 minimum. Third-party costs can also apply.

AXIOM

0.95% net at base tier

Axiom's fee docs list a 0.95% net fee at the Wood tier after 0.05% cashback, with lower net fees at higher reward tiers. Network and execution settings can add costs.

SMALL TRADES

Feature fit

Where each one wins.

ONBOARDING

FOMO

Best if you want email/Apple-style onboarding, mobile convenience and a product designed to feel less like a specialist terminal.

EXECUTION CONTROL

Axiom

Best if priority fees, slippage, MEV modes, migration actions and detailed execution settings are part of your edge.

SOCIAL CONTEXT

FOMO

Best if trader profiles, feeds, theses, alerts and leaderboards are central to how you discover ideas.

WALLET / TWITTER TRACKING

Axiom

Axiom explicitly builds wallet tracking and Twitter monitoring into its trading workflow.

MULTICHAIN

FOMO

FOMO positions itself around supported multichain trading from one balance, reducing bridge friction for newcomers.

LEARNING CURVE

Depends on you

FOMO hides more complexity. Axiom exposes more controls. Simpler is not automatically better; advanced is not automatically better either.
