For meme-coin traders

When a hardware signer starts making sense.

01

You are holding, not flipping.

If assets are meant to sit for longer, separating storage from the trading interface can reduce exposure to a single app or hot-wallet environment.

02

You understand recovery responsibility.

Self-custody shifts responsibility to you. Losing or exposing recovery material can still lead to permanent loss.

03

You verify every signature.

Hardware does not make malicious approvals safe. Read what you sign and keep recovery material offline and private.
