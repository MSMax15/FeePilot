Six things a newcomer should know.

01

The feed is the center.

FOMO describes its social feed as a place to see top traders, buys, sells and changing position status.

02

Search is broader than coins.

You can search assets and trader profiles, then use profile context to decide what deserves more research.

03

Funding depends on platform.

FOMO's current guidance lists Apple Pay, debit card and crypto funding in mobile, while new web users are directed to crypto funding or the mobile app for card onramps.

FOMO funding guide →
04

Fees matter.

Current terms list at least 0.50% per buy/sell transaction with a $0.95 minimum. Frequent small trades can add up.

FOMO fees explained →
05

Easy execution is still real risk.

One-click buying and simple onboarding reduce friction. They do not make meme coins or leveraged trades safer.

06

Social signals can mislead.

Leaderboards and alerts can surface ideas quickly, but other traders' visible results do not transfer their timing, sizing or edge to you.
