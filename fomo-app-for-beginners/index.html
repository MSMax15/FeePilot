FOMO app for beginners

FOMO presents itself as a fast social crypto trading app with viral-token discovery, feeds, alerts and one-click buying. It also supports broader crypto and perpetual futures. If you are new, the key is understanding which product you are using before you trade.

1. Know the fee model

FOMO's current Terms state a minimum 0.50% fee per buy/sell transaction, subject to a $0.95 minimum fee. Perps carry a 0.05% FOMO fee plus possible third-party costs.

2. Referral codes reduce fees, not risk

FOMO's public referral pages currently show a 10% fee reduction for entering a referral code in-app. SaveOnFees features LMP506. Verify the live benefit before trading.

3. Meme coins can go to zero

FOMO's own educational material warns that memecoin trading is highly speculative and users should only invest what they can afford to lose completely.

4. Spot and perps are not the same

Spot means buying the asset itself. Perpetuals are leveraged derivatives and can involve liquidation and funding costs. If you do not understand leverage, avoid treating perps like ordinary token purchases.

5. First-trade checklist

  • Confirm the asset and product type.
  • Check the live fee before submitting.
  • Confirm any referral benefit.
  • Start with an amount you can afford to lose.
  • Do not chase a token just because it is trending.
  • Know how withdrawals and wallet access work.
