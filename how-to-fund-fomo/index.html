Funding methods

Start with the device you are using.

APPLE PAY

Mobile app route.

FOMO's current Apple Pay guidance says to open the mobile app, go to Add Funds, choose Apple Pay and confirm through the device payment flow. If Apple Pay is missing or declined, use the troubleshooting guide rather than repeatedly retrying.

Apple Pay troubleshooting →
DEBIT CARD

Mobile card onramp.

FOMO's public app guidance lists debit card as a mobile funding option. Availability, bank approval, limits and third-party terms can vary, so use the live in-app flow as the source of truth.

CRYPTO

Use the shown deposit instructions.

FOMO's deposit guidance describes choosing the crypto deposit option, selecting a supported network and sending only the asset/network combination the live app shows. Older FOMO guidance specifically referenced USDC on supported networks, so verify current support before sending.

Do not guess a network or asset. Crypto transfers can be irreversible. Copy the current address from FOMO, verify the supported asset and network in the live product, and consider a small test transfer where appropriate.
Using FOMO on desktop?

Web funding is not the same flow.

FOMO's April 2026 web announcement says a new web user can fund with crypto to start trading, while Apple Pay and debit-card onramps are handled through the mobile app. If a payment option is missing on desktop, that does not automatically mean it is unavailable in the mobile product.

Before funding

Funding cost and trading fee are different layers.

FOMO's terms say third-party fees may apply. A card/onramp cost, network cost or bank restriction should not be confused with FOMO's published buy/sell fee schedule.

Quick answers

Funding FOMO without guessing.

Can I use Apple Pay?

FOMO currently documents Apple Pay funding in the mobile app. If it is not working, check mobile vs web, limits and your bank before assuming the app is down.

Can I use a debit card?

FOMO's public app guidance lists debit-card funding in the mobile app. Bank and provider restrictions can still affect a transaction.

Can I deposit crypto?

Yes, FOMO documents crypto deposits. Use only the asset, address and network the current app shows; support can change.

What about FOMO web?

FOMO's current web-launch guidance points new web users to crypto funding and the mobile app for Apple Pay or debit-card onramps.

Does LMP506 change funding fees?

We do not claim that it does. The referral benefit FOMO advertises should be checked separately from third-party funding and onramp costs.

What if a deposit is pending?

Stop retrying if money appears charged or pending. Check the provider status and contact the relevant support channel with only the transaction details it is safe to share.
